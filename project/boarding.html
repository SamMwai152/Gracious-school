Boarding at Gracious

A home away from home

Gracious School Lanet is a mixed day and boarding school. Our boarding section is designed to feel like a warm, caring home for your child. We provide a safe environment with caring supervision, a structured daily routine, and staff who look after each child's well-being.

Boarders benefit from a supportive atmosphere where they can focus on their studies, make friends, and grow in character — all while feeling valued and cared for.
