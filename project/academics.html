Our Curriculum

Competency-Based Education (CBE)

At Gracious Schools Lanet, we follow the Competency-Based Education (CBE) approach. This means your child doesn't just memorise facts — they develop practical skills, creativity, critical thinking, communication, and good values. Learning is hands-on and meaningful, helping children understand how to apply what they learn in real life. Each child is supported at their own pace, building confidence as they grow.

Our Facilities

Learning spaces that inspire

Our facilities give pupils the opportunity to learn by doing.

Facility

Science Laboratory

Our science laboratory is equipped to give pupils hands-on experience with experiments and scientific discovery. Children learn by observing, questioning, and experimenting — building curiosity and a love for science.

Facility

Computer Laboratory

Our computer laboratory helps pupils develop digital literacy from an early age. With computers available for hands-on practice, children learn essential computing skills that prepare them for the modern world.

Facility

Home Science Laboratory

Our home science laboratory provides a practical space for pupils to learn life skills — from food preparation to basic craft and care skills. These hands-on lessons build independence and creativity.
