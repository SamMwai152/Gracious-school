Who We Are

A school where every child matters

Gracious Schools Lanet is a mixed day and boarding primary school in Lanet, Nakuru County, offering Competency-Based Education. We aim to provide a nurturing environment where learning, creativity, and character grow together, shaping bright minds and building confident, future leaders.

Our Motto

"Step into a school where excellence is a standard, not an option!"
