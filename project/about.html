Who We Are

A school where every child matters

Gracious Schools Lanet is a mixed day and boarding school in Lanet, Nakuru County, offering Competency-Based Education. We aim to provide a nurturing environment where learning, creativity, and character grow together, shaping bright minds and building confident, future leaders.

Our Story

The journey of Gracious Schools Lanet

The Foundation

Our Humble Beginnings: Gracious Schools Lanet officially opened its doors to its first batch of eager learners, establishing a foundation rooted in strong moral values and quality academic standards.

Holistic Growth & Boarding Facilities

Expanding Horizons: To accommodate learners from further regions and provide a fully immersive educational experience, the school introduced modern boarding facilities alongside its day school system.

Investing in Innovation

State-of-the-Art Labs Installed: To champion practical and tech-driven education, the school built and fully equipped its Advanced Science Lab, Computer Lab, and Home Science Lab.

Broadening Co-Curricular Excellence

Beyond the Classroom: The formal introduction of talent-nurturing clubs, including skating, taekwondo, scouts, and specialized music programs, ensuring our pupils grow holistically.

Adapting to the Future (CBC Integration): 2023 – Present

The Transition to Junior School: Embracing Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC), the school successfully pioneered classes up to Junior School (Grade 9), celebrating major graduation milestones for PP2, Grade 6, and Grade 9 learners.
